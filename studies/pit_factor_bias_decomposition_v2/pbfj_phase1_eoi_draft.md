**Subject:** Expression of Interest - Hybrid Pre-Registration - Publication Timing and the A-Share ROE Signal

Dear Professor Faff and the Pacific-Basin Finance Journal editorial team,

We seek guidance on the suitability of a hybrid pre-registration proposal titled **"Report Dates, Publication Dates, and the A-Share ROE Signal: A Pre-Specified Historical Confirmation."**

The project asks a focused measurement question in empirical asset pricing: **how much of the measured A-share ROE-return relation remains after the accounting signal is withheld until the first common official signal session strictly after its stored report publication date?** The proposed contribution is not a new factor or a search for the best-performing specification. The primary comparison is paired within month and within a historical listing universe; an ordered common-support identity then distinguishes changes in usable securities from changes in the selected accounting record. A complete implementation lattice is retained as supporting sensitivity analysis rather than presented as the paper's central novelty.

We have already completed and publicly disclosed a short pilot using 2023-2026 data and covering 18 evaluation cross-sections from January 2025 through June 2026. The pilot was repository-locked but not externally preregistered. It reports the complete observed 4×4 pilot matrix—16 factor–variant cells—and shows that recorded-publication alignment reduced the observed mean ROE and fixed-composite rank ICs. We recognize that the entire pilot is observed prior information. It will be reported separately as an empirical precondition and will not enter the Stage-2 primary estimate or any claim of prospective confirmation.

Our proposed Stage-2 design treats the pilot only as a transparent empirical precondition. Before accessing Stage-2 outcomes, we would freeze and externally timestamp:

- one primary estimand: the paired monthly signed ROE rank-IC decrement, defined as publication-date IC minus report-period-end IC within the point-in-time universe;
- an ordered three-part decomposition of that total ROE contrast for potentially non-nested signal supports: report-side support restriction, within-common-support record replacement, and publication-side support extension, with the monthly identity enforced to absolute tolerance `1e-12`;
- a SH/SZ A-share historical outcome panel targeting January 2010 through December 2022, with no factor return, IC, ranking, or variant outcome computed, released, or human-inspected before a signed exposure attestation, external registration, and custodian release authorization;
- the fixed-weight composite as a secondary timing estimand and momentum and low volatility as timing negative controls;
- a complete 2^4 factorial comprising 16 implementation variants and 64 implementation cells; together with the two report-end comparators, the design contains 18 variants and 72 factor–variant reporting cells overall;
- exact Shapley attribution limited to that four-component implementation block, paired HAC inference, exactly 29 inferential estimands (one primary plus a fixed 28-test secondary Benjamini-Hochberg family, including the three non-directional ROE support components), two separate deterministic timing-isolation checks, the deterministic common-support efficiency identity, and all-cell reporting;
- a prior-exposure log, with a structured deviation-log and receipt-reporting module explicitly planned but not yet implemented;
- a code, plan, file-hash, and result receipt.

The 2010-2022 target interval is a fixed design boundary, not a menu from which a favorable subperiod can be selected. The immutable bounded source-probe v1 remains unchanged; its prospective v2 successor must be externally timestamped before any bounded probe is executed. If the outcome-blind coverage, publication-date, membership, endpoint, rights, prior-exposure, or code gates fail, the Stage-2 study stops; the team will not shorten the interval after seeing outcomes. No reviewed delivery currently satisfies the complete historical, semantic, and rights gates, and no 2010-2022 factor-outcome panel has been assembled. A locally retained pilot export is described as licensed in the legacy repository record, but its provider-derived public metadata and controlled-review permissions have not yet been re-attested under the new Stage-2 rights packet. Historical outcomes will be released to the analysis runner only after the protocol is frozen, externally registered, independently verified, and separately authorized by the data custodian. Automated pre-lock validation may parse raw numeric bytes to check schema, finiteness, dates, identifiers, exact endpoints, and aggregate coverage; it is forbidden to compute or expose factor returns, ranks, ICs, portfolio results, or variant orderings. The raw data are proprietary; subject to written rights, we propose public schemas, extraction specifications, synthetic fixtures, code, immutable hashes, permitted aggregate coverage, a one-command authorized rerun, and an independent licensed replication or reviewer-access arrangement.

The current Stage-2 IC core covers the complete cell matrix, paired IC estimands, HAC inference, the four-component IC Shapley decomposition, the ordered ROE common-support decomposition, aggregate missingness/support counts, and no-return publication-exposure diagnostics, including calendar-day reporting-delay summaries. The 72 cell means, t-statistics, and top-minus-universe spreads are complete descriptive outputs, not 72 discovery tests; confirmatory inference is restricted to the sole primary and fixed BH-28 family. The signal-eligible denominator is fixed before outcome lookup. Required official-session endpoints must be either provider-recorded `traded_close` observations for non-suspended rows or supplier-recorded/published same-session `suspension_valuation` observations for suspended rows; the research code cannot forward-fill either path. These outputs are valuation returns and rank ICs, not executable trade returns. An unresolved endpoint makes the cell non-estimable and the study `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, rather than allowing a security to be dropped, moved to its next quote, valued at an unattested last price, or assigned a default recovery. The result receipt binds a complete private per-security endpoint-reason ledger by hash and publishes only aggregate reason counts. The delisting-terminal-wealth adapter, suspension execution/fill assumptions, full non-endpoint exclusion attribution, percentage attenuation, raw-ratio regressions, robustness analyses, next-open portfolios, transaction costs, nonfills, stationary bootstrap intervals, factorial interaction tests, and announcement-event/return-timing studies remain excluded unless separately completed, tested, and frozen. The full 18-variant, 72-cell design remains blocked unless the supplier proves same-session suspension-valuation provenance; only before external registration and any Stage-2 outcome access may a prospectively amended and re-frozen suspension-off design retain 10 variants and 40 cells. The Shapley allocation preserves interactions inside the four-component block; it does not constitute a formal interaction test.

Because the proposed fundamental input is a single-version export, the strongest authorized statement is a recorded-publication-date specification effect: the retained snapshot record becomes eligible only on the first common official signal session strictly after its recorded publication date. The design cannot identify the ROE value investors observed at first release and will not claim revision history, vintage-value effects, restatement effects, or announcement-return reactions.

If the journal invites a later phase, the frozen execution-plan core would first be bound in a non-self-referential design manifest; an external provider receipt would then bind that manifest hash; and a separate execution authorization would verify the chronology before blind-data release. Only then would the final plan envelope record those backward-pointing hashes and `locked_at`, without changing the registered core. The current runner deliberately accepts only a named human verifier and hash-bound provider record as an explicit trust boundary; it does not claim to validate cryptographic signatures or inclusion proofs. This EOI remains an unsent draft and none of those steps has occurred.

2. At which phase of the hybrid pathway should the independent custodian release the sealed 2010-2022 panel, and does that require Phase-3 in-principle acceptance rather than an external timestamp alone?
3. What reviewer-access arrangement is expected when licensed market data cannot be redistributed?
4. May the hybrid pathway be considered for the current Responsible and Open Science initiative, or should it proceed through the journal's general preregistration route?
5. Should the proposed submission remain an IC-core study, or would the journal expect the planned portfolio, bootstrap, and interaction modules to be implemented before a Phase-3 Preregistered Report?

We would be pleased to provide the draft statistical analysis plan, pilot receipt, code repository, coverage-gate report, and a concise research pitch.
